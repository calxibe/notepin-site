Explore the user manual ↗
01

Capture without losing your place

Highlight a passage and choose Save to NotePin from the right-click menu. Or write your own note. The page address comes with it.

02

A small note, or the whole collection

Open the toolbar popup for a quick thought, keep the side panel beside your browsing, or use the note board to search by site, tag, and color.

03

Pick up where you left off

Turn on optional page stickies to see your notes when you return to a site. Pin important notes, use eight colors, and edit or organize several at once.

04

Your notes travel with you

Notes sync when browser sync is enabled. Export a backup or move your notes with JSON import. If sync is incomplete, NotePin keeps your saved copy safe.
